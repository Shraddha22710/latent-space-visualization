What is happening?

The high-dimensional points are generated from a simple 3D shape with optional extra noise dimensions. The selected method turns them into a 2D map.

Algorithm recipe

Center the data, find the directions of maximum variance, then project each point onto the top two principal components.

How to read it

Colors follow the hidden position along the original dataset. In PCA, far directions are meaningful. In SNE, t-SNE, and UMAP, trust neighborhoods more than global axes.
